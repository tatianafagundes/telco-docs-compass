# Product: ERAD document management for telecom

ERAD is a multi-tenant document management service for a telecom group. Each country is a tenant under a master group. Documents belong to telecom records (orders, quotes, contracts, accounts, customers, tickets, sites). They are added, have their details confirmed by a person, are approved under four-eyes, kept per retention rules or legal hold, shared carefully, and exposed to connected systems through TMF667 v4.

Experience principles:
- Customer first, calm, Apple-like.
- Every unavailable action says why.
- Undo is preferred to confirmation.
- No folders: documents are found through records and search.
- Sentence case. Warm, plain English.

Read `docs/rebuild/00-README.md` first. The reference behaviour is in `docs/rebuild/03-business-rules.md` and `docs/rebuild/05-screens/`.
